# frontend/

The demo UI. The screens use **AITM** (AI Transfer Money), a fictional payments brand defined only in `frontend/brand.ts` (no Paytm logo or name in the UI; Paytm stays the company we pitch to, in `docs/`). Kavach is the feature: "Kavach by AITM". `src/app/page.tsx` renders the single-screen version; `src/app/{customer,soundbox,merchant,bank}/page.tsx` render the four live screens (issue #4, `docs/multi-screen-demo.md`).

## The four live screens (branch `feat/multi-screen-demo`)

All four poll the shared state (SQLite, `backend/db/`) through `useLive(role)` and receive only their role's snapshot. A reset from any screen bumps `epoch`, and every screen returns to its start state.

- `useLive.ts` — `useLive<S>(role, { intervalMs, onReset })` returns `{ state, error, setError, busy, call }`. Polls `POST /api/kavach {action: "state", role}`; `call(body)` adds `role` to every action. `onReset` fires when `epoch` changes.
- `speech.ts` — one queue for everything spoken. `enqueue(text, lang, useSarvam, englishFallback?)` starts fetching Sarvam audio (`/api/voice/tts` with `language`) at once and plays phrases strictly one after another through one `AudioContext`, so overlapping payments never cut each other off. `prefetch` caches decoded audio by language and text (failures are not cached). If Sarvam is off, rate-limited, failing or slower than 6 s, the browser voice for that language speaks (`od-IN` → `or-IN`); if the device has no voice for it, the English fallback is said in `en-IN`. `pauseSpeech`/`resumeSpeech` (pausing cuts the current phrase, which is said again on resume, drops queued prompts — `enqueue(..., { prompt: true })` — and keeps fetching; a phrase whose audio arrives while paused waits), a context that is not running falls back to the browser voice, and a watchdog (audio length + 1.5 s) keeps one phrase from stalling the queue, `onSpeaking(listener)`, `stopSpeaking` (drops the queue), `unlockAudio` (call inside a tap; it creates the shared AudioContext). `speak(text, useSarvam, lang = "hi-IN")` is kept for the merchant screen.
- `CustomerApp.tsx` (`/customer`) — buyer's Scan & Pay (AITM) (branch `feat/scan-qr`): home → camera scanner (`QrScanner`) → amount → UPI PIN (any 4 digits, then ✓) → green-tick success. A static shop QR gives an editable amount; a bill QR (has `tr`) calls `bill-lookup` first, shows the server's amount read-only and refuses if the bill is not `open` or the QR's `am` differs. Pays with `pay {amountInr, payerVpa}` or `pay {billId, amountInr, payerVpa}`; the synthetic `DEMO_PAYER_VPA` is masked server-side. "Can't scan? Pick the shop" skips the camera (₹826 prefilled). Shows nothing about holds or reports.
- `QrScanner.tsx` — full-screen "Scan any QR": `getUserMedia({facingMode: environment})`, frames downscaled to 480 px and decoded with `jsqr` in a `requestAnimationFrame` loop; camera stopped on unmount (also when unmounted while the permission prompt is open). `onDecode(text)` returns `null` to accept or a message to show while scanning continues. Also "Upload QR image" (decodes a photo or screenshot) and the fallback button. Needs HTTPS on phones (`npm run dev:lan`).
- `upi.ts` — `buildUpiUri` / `parseUpiUri(text, expectedVpa)` for standard UPI URIs. Static: `upi://pay?pa=<vpa>&pn=<shop>&cu=INR`. Bill: plus `am=826.00&tr=<billId>&tn=Bill%20<billId>` (no sold items in the QR). Parse refuses non-`upi://pay`, another payee, non-INR, bad amounts (≤0, over ₹1,00,000, more than 2 decimals) and odd `tr`. Tests: `tests/upi.test.ts` (includes a `qrcode` → `jsqr` round trip).
- `QrCode.tsx` — `<QrCode text size />`, inline SVG from `qrcode`'s module matrix (error correction M, quiet zone 2). Prints cleanly; no `<img>`.
- `ShopStandee.tsx` (`/qr`) — printable counter standee with the static shop QR, payee from the customer snapshot. Marked "DEMO · not a real payment QR". "Print standee" hides itself in print.
- `BillPanel.tsx` (`/merchant/bill`; no props, to be mounted in the merchant app too) — amount + "क्या बेचा?" → `bill-create`; shows the bill QR big, the bill id and live status (open → paid ✓ / cancelled) from `state.bills`; `bill-cancel`; recent bills. Polls as `merchant` and never renders hold data. Payee VPA comes from `DEMO_PAYEE_VPA` (the merchant snapshot has no VPA).
- Brand: `brand.ts` is the single source of truth: `BRAND` (`name` "AITM", `payName`, `business`, `hi` "एआईटीएम", `expansion` "AI Transfer Money", `tagline`, `kavach`, palette `primary` sky blue #00AEEF / `navy` #002E6E / `accent` saffron #F59E0B, `vpaDomain`, `spokenOn` per language) and `brandCssVars()` (CSS variables, Tailwind `brand-*` colours). `BrandLogo.tsx` is the original logo (`variant` full / mark / white); also `public/brand/logo.svg` and `src/app/icon.svg`. Never hard-code the brand name or its colours elsewhere; to rename, edit `brand.ts` only. Every branded screen keeps a "Demo" label.
- `SoundboxApp.tsx` (`/soundbox`) — a Soundbox mock (`BrandLogo` with a "Demo" label, LED, LCD with the last amount, speaker grille) above a touch panel. Needs one "Tap to switch on". Speaks in the snapshot's `language` (merchant setting) using `voice/announce.ts`. Announces every payment that arrives after power-on, oldest first, queued; a bill payment adds "bill N paid" and is not asked about; one "what did you sell?" follows a batch with an open payment. The fixed phrases are prefetched per language, so each payment costs one TTS call.
  - **Open payments** (`canTag`, not `billId`; a bill with no text is still open) are cards with live countdowns. One big mic: hold to talk (release stops) or tap once (stops on 1.2 s of silence, a second tap, or 7 s); a level meter; status "Listening / Understanding / Saving". Speech is paused while recording and transcribing.
  - A voice tag goes `/api/voice/stt` (with `language`) → `tag-auto`. `bound` → saved; `ambiguous` → the candidate cards light up (candidates missing from the snapshot's five newest payments are added) and a tap calls `tag {txnId, transcript}` (the transcript is kept if that fails); `none_open` → message. Tapping a card first, then speaking, tags that card directly. Without a key, or once the mic has failed to start (no permission, no device, plain http on a phone): "Use recorded clip", or a card tap tags `DEMO_PAYMENT.spokenTag`. The mic stays enabled, and a later successful start returns to voice.
  - Recent list: tagged, bill-tagged, under-threshold and "window closed". Never shows reports or holds.
- `KavachApp.tsx` with `controls={false}` (`/merchant`) — same merchant app without the demo-controls panel. It reacts to a hold placed from the bank screen (fetches and speaks the explanation once per hold) and fetches the escalation draft once the tracker reaches `escalation_due`. The hold alert has wording for both scopes: `lien` ("₹826 पर रोक") and `debit_freeze` ("खाते से निकासी पर रोक", complaint on one payment).
- Merchant app tabs (`frontend/merchant/`): Home, Payments, **Proofs** (`proofs.tsx`: every payment with its proof ID `KVC-<txn>` (same as the certificate), transaction id, UTR, bill id, masked payer, the words recorded at payment time, how they were matched (`boundBy`), the invoice fingerprint, "Copy proof record", and "Attach invoice photo / PDF" → `POST /api/invoice` while `canAttachInvoice`), Bills (`bills.tsx`, with the bill UPI QR) and Soundbox (`soundbox.tsx`, voice language). The certificate card (`parts.tsx`) shows how the tag was bound and the invoice block.
- `BankConsole.tsx` (`/bank`) — presenter's laptop. 1) Report a payment (victim on 1930/NCRP) → ack number. 2) Apply a restriction: `lien` (default, MHA SOP s.9.1) or `debit_freeze` (over-reach case). 3) Simulate day `tracker.escalateAfterDays` (22). 4) Law watcher buttons. Also "Reset whole demo" and links to the phone screens.

## What belongs here

- **Payment screen** — a payment lands, the announcement is spoken, the merchant tags the sale by voice.
- **Sales list** — today's payments with their tags, or why they are untagged.
- **Hold alert** — the screen turns red for a hold, showing the held amount (for example ₹826) and making clear that only that amount is held.
- **Explanation view** — the plain-language Hindi explanation, with a play button when speech is on.
- **Mule screen** — two yes/no questions before any documents are prepared.
- **Packet view** — the sale certificate and the drafted grievance, with copy and download. The merchant files it themselves. Share is not built yet.
- **Tracker** — where the grievance stands on the ladder, days elapsed, and the escalation draft when due.
- **Demo controls** — trigger a payment, trigger a hold, "simulate day 22", reset, and the law-watch panel.
- A merchant switcher is not built yet (the `merchant` API action exists but no UI calls it).

## What is here now

- `KavachApp.tsx` — the whole client app (`"use client"`). Holds UI state and calls `POST /api/kavach` with actions `state`, `pay`, `tag`, `hold`, then `explain`, `packet`, `filed`, `advance` then `escalation`, `reset`, `check-feeds`, `simulate-law-change`. Each reply's `state` (`Snapshot` from `backend/api.ts`) replaces local state. Voice: `useRecorder` → `POST /api/voice/stt` → `tag`; announcements and the explanation are spoken via `POST /api/voice/tts` only when `speechConfigured`. Internal components `LawWatch` and `Question`.
- The "Simulate day N" button advances the clock by `tracker.escalateAfterDays - daysSinceFiled` (22 days with today's ladder), then requests the escalation draft. Its handler is still named `day15` and the label falls back to 15 before a hold exists; the real figure comes from the ladder.
- `parts.tsx` — `inr`, `when` (browser-local time), `Disclaimer`, `Citations` (status label, source link, "Checked <date>" or "Not yet verified", and "Possibly changed, under review" alerts), `CertificateCard` (separate "From <brand> records" and "Said by the merchant at payment time" sections), `DraftCard` (Draft badge, "Worded by AI" vs "Standard wording", Copy, Download with disclaimer appended), type `AlertsByRule`.

## Rules for this folder

- Every screen showing a draft or legal information displays: draft, not legal advice, no guaranteed release.
- Each rule shown carries its source and last-checked date.
- No "submit to bank" or "send" button that implies Kavach files anything. Actions are review, copy, download (and share when built). "मैंने इसे अपने बैंक में जमा कर दिया है" only records that the merchant filed it.
- Show masked buyer details and last four account digits only.
- Hindi first, large text, minimal typing. Design for a shopkeeper at a counter.
- The hold screen must reassure before it instructs: a hold on one payment, not an accusation.
- Import server code only as types. Never import `voice/sarvam.ts` or call Sarvam from the browser.
